# ResChew Route
Reduce food waste by rescuing and delivering surplus food to those in need before it expires!

# Description
ResChew Route is an innovative platform designed to reduce food waste and support communities in need! Our application connects restaurants with surplus food to food banks and drivers who can help deliver it.
First, restaurants submit posts about surplus food to the global database. The food banks receive these posts, accept these donations, and request for drivers in their area to deliver the food to them. Finally, drivers can accept tasks opened by the food banks and make the connection between restaurants and food banks happen!
