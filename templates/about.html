About

Introducing ResChew Route, an application that orchestrates the rescue and delivery of food before it expires!


OUR MISSION
ResChew Route is dedicated to saving the environment by reducing food waste and greenhouse gas emissions from landfills, while also addressing food insecurity and world hunger.

This platform ensures accessibility and efficiency when connecting restaurants, food banks, and delivery drivers through posts, orders, and tasks, in real-time!

Not sure where to head next?

  • The Issue: Learn more about the global issue of food waste and how it is a detriment to the environment, community sustainability, and world hunger
  • Our Work: Discover ResChew Route's solution to this problem and our impact
  • Take Action: click on Restaurant, Food Bank, or Delivery to be directed to your respective dashboard
  • Contact: Send inquiries, comments, or feedback

Technologies

  • Map Box API for map visual and distance calculation
  • Database to store history of rescued food
  • Flask for backend
