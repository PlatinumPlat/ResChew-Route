How Food Gets to the Food Bank

Restaurants, food banks, and delivery drivers use the site to share updates and move extra food before it expires

01

Restaurant Posts Food

The restaurant posts what food is available, how much there is, where it is located, and when it expires

Post is live
02

Food Bank Accepts

The food bank gets a notification, checks the post, and accepts the food if it can use and distribute it

Notification sent
03

Driver Picks It Up

A delivery driver sees the accepted task, claims the pickup, and takes the food from the restaurant to the food bank

Handoff complete

Everyone Sees the Updates

Each person can see the latest status, so the restaurant, food bank, and driver know what needs to happen next
