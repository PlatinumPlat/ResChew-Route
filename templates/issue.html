The Issue

Food waste is a major global issue that worsens hunger and food insecurity as countless nutritious meals are thrown out while millions of individuals around the globe have limited or no access to food as a result of poverty and food shortages.

According to the UN...

25-30% ~1.6 Billion tons

of total food produced is wasted every year

The food service sector was responsible for

290 million tons

of food wasted in 2022

More than

1 billion meals

worth of edible food is wasted every day.

In the US, wasted food makes up almost

40% of the food supply

and around 60% of it is sent to landfills, where it releases the greenhouse gas methane into the atmosphere

Food losses vary by category, reaching

40-50% for fruits and vegetables

while cereals lose roughly 30%.

If food loss and waste were a country, it would be the

third-largest greenhouse gas emitter

in the world.

Statistics adapted from Earth.Org's food waste facts.
